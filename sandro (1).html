04 / The essentials

Learn in 60 seconds

Click a topic to reveal the simple explanation.

01

Inflation

Inflation means prices generally rise over time, so the same amount of money may buy fewer goods later.

02

Compound growth

When returns are added to your balance, future returns can be earned on both the original amount and earlier growth.

03

Diversification

Diversification means spreading money across different assets or investments rather than relying on only one.
